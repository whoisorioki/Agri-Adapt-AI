# Cloudoon Stakeholder Presentation: Agri-Adapt AI

## Executive Summary Slide Deck

### Slide 1: The Crisis of Choice
**Title**: "From Data Overload to Decisive Action"

**The Problem**:
- Kenya's 5 million smallholder farmers face an **information paradox**
- **Data Abundance**: Complex dashboards, conflicting forecasts, technical reports
- **Decision Urgency**: Simple question: "What should I plant, and when?"
- **Result**: Information paralysis → Reactive decisions → Reduced yields → Food insecurity

**Visual**: Split screen showing overwhelming data dashboard vs. simple farmer question

### Slide 2: Our Vision - The "One-Number" Strategy
**Title**: "Holistic Agricultural Resilience Score: Clarity from Complexity"

**The Solution**:
- **Single Metric**: 0-100 resilience score for any sub-county, any season
- **Example**: "Baringo County maize resilience this season: 78/100"
- **Meaning**: Clear, actionable guidance integrating all critical factors

**Three Pillars**:
1. **Climate Hazard Exposure** (40%): Environmental risks
2. **Agricultural Vulnerability** (30%): Farming system sensitivity  
3. **Socioeconomic Adaptive Capacity** (30%): Community resilience

**Visual**: Simple score display with three-pillar breakdown
