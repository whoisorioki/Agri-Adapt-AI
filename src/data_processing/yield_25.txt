## KEY INSIGHTS

1. **Highest Productivity**: Irish Potatoes and Cassava lead in yields per hectare
2. **Lowest Productivity**: Millet and Beans show lowest yields, reflecting ASAL growing conditions
3. **Regional Variations**: High-potential areas (Trans Nzoia, Uasin Gishu, Nakuru) show consistently higher maize yields
4. **Climate Impact**: 2024 yields affected by erratic rainfall patterns and flooding
5. **Top Performers 2024**:
   - Maize: Uasin Gishu, Trans Nzoia, Kericho, Nandi
   - Potatoes: Bomet, Uasin Gishu, Nakuru, West Pokot
   - Cassava: Lamu, Kilifi, Busia, Homa Bay
